# Example: REWRITE

> All names and companies are fictional. The input is intentionally weak.

## Input

**Subject:** Quick Question About Your Onboarding Process

Hi Dana,

I hope you're doing well. I'm reaching out because we help SaaS companies improve onboarding with our AI-powered platform. Our solution streamlines the entire process, reduces manual work, and gives CS teams real-time visibility into account health.

We've worked with many companies in your space and consistently delivered strong results. I'd love to show you how we can help Northwind reduce churn and increase expansion revenue.

Would you be open to a 30-minute call next week? If not, feel free to reply with your thoughts or check out our website to learn more.

Best,
Alex

## Short checklist of the original

- **Length:** PASS (about 100 words).
- **First line:** FAIL - starts with "I hope you're doing well", then "I'm reaching out".
- **Pitch:** FAIL - AI-powered platform, streamline, visibility, account health.
- **Problem:** FAIL - no concrete problem, only "improve onboarding".
- **CTA:** FAIL - a 30-minute call, a reply, and a website link.
- **Pronouns:** FAIL - "we/our/I" outnumbers "you".
- **Language:** FAIL - jargon and an unverifiable claim about results.
- **Subject:** FAIL - six generic words in title case.
- **Voice:** FAIL - reads like a template with the name swapped.

**Original: 1/11**

## Rewritten

**Subject:** week one

Dana - your post about 40% of new accounts never finishing setup stayed with me. Most teams only see it when the account goes quiet.

We make a small checklist that shows where each account gets stuck, day by day.

Is that still a problem, or is setup under control?

Yevhen, Founder at Relay

**Words:** 53

## What changed

- Kept the true fact from the brief: Dana's post about setup drop-off.
- Cut the platform, the features, the social proof, and the second and third asks.
- Replaced the call request with one question that costs one word to answer.
- Shortened the subject to the recipient's world, not the sender's process.

## What did not change

The offer is the same. The rewrite did not invent a new product, a customer, or a result. It removed everything that was not needed to earn a reply.
