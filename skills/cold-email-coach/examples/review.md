# Example: REVIEW

> The email below is fictional and intentionally bad. All names and companies are invented.

## Input email

**Subject:** Revolutionize Your Customer Success with Our AI-Powered Platform!!!

Hi [First Name],

I hope this email finds you well. My name is Alex Morgan and I am the VP of Sales at InnovateStack, a leading provider of AI-powered customer success solutions. I wanted to reach out because I believe our platform could be a game-changer for your team.

At InnovateStack, we leverage cutting-edge artificial intelligence to streamline the entire customer journey. Our robust, end-to-end platform integrates seamlessly with your existing stack, automates manual follow-ups, provides real-time analytics, and empowers CS teams to drive outcomes. We are trusted by world-class companies and have helped them achieve 10x ROI.

We understand that companies like yours face unique challenges when it comes to onboarding and retention. That is why we built a solution that is customizable, scalable, and easy to implement. Our platform can help you reduce churn, increase expansion revenue, and improve customer satisfaction across the board.

I would love to schedule a 30-minute demo to show you how InnovateStack can transform your customer success organization. Alternatively, you can reply to this email with your thoughts, or book a time directly on my calendar here: [link].

I know you're busy, so I'll keep this short. Please don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions.

Best regards,
Alex Morgan
VP of Sales, InnovateStack

## Review

1. **Length - FAIL.** 213 words. More than double the ceiling, and the useful content could fit in two sentences.
2. **First line - FAIL.** "I hope this email finds you well" is about the sender's manners, and the next sentence introduces the sender, not the recipient.
3. **No pitch - FAIL.** The email lists integrations, automation, analytics, customization, scalability, and ease of implementation. It is a feature dump.
4. **Problem first - FAIL.** The only problem mentioned is generic churn and retention. Nothing specific to Dana's team or their onboarding process.
5. **One CTA - FAIL.** Three asks: a 30-minute demo, a reply with thoughts, and a calendar link. Attention splits three ways.
6. **You over me - FAIL.** "We/our/I" dominates. "You" appears twice, both in vague phrases ("your team", "your thoughts").
7. **Plain language - FAIL.** Leverage, streamline, robust, end-to-end, seamlessly, empower, outcomes, customizable, scalable. The email reads like a brochure.
8. **Subject line - FAIL.** Eleven words, title case, an exclamation mark, and a promise the email cannot keep.
9. **No clichés - FAIL.** "Hope this email finds you well", "I wanted to reach out", "world-class", "companies like yours", "I know you're busy", "please don't hesitate".
10. **No pressure - FAIL.** "10x ROI", "game-changer", "transform your organization". Claims without evidence, aimed at creating excitement.
11. **Human voice - FAIL.** It sounds assembled from a template. No one speaks like this.

**Score: 0/11**

**Top fixes**

1. Replace the first two paragraphs with one specific observation about Dana's onboarding process, then one sentence about what Relay does.
2. Cut from 213 to about 70 words. Keep one problem, one sentence of context, one question.
3. Remove the demo, the calendar link, and "reply with your thoughts". End with a single low-bar question such as "Is this still a problem, or is setup under control?"
